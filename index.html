CARD-CROSSING WORKSHOP

Design toy bridges with card crossings.

Archwright is a workshop-bench puzzle game. Every crossing begins with a shuffled deck. Tap two adjacent cards to swap them, line up same-color neighbors, and watch a stable arch lock into place across each row.

FAQ

A few quick answers. For anything else, write to dhfu94086@gmail.com.

Does Archwright require an account or internet connection?

No. Archwright has no login flow and never makes a network call. Everything lives on your device.

Which permissions does the app request?

Only Camera and Photo Library, and only when you choose to attach a photo to a saved bridge. You can deny either and the rest of the app still works.

How do I erase my data?

Open Workshop Settings and tap Erase All Workshop Data. The action is irreversible.

Where can I get help?

Email dhfu94086@gmail.com. We usually respond within two business days.
