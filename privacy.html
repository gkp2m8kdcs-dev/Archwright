PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · App name: Archwright · Contact: dhfu94086@gmail.com

Short version: Archwright is an offline-first app. It does not require an account, does not contact our servers, and does not collect analytics. All saved bridges, photos, and statistics live on your device. You can erase everything with a single tap in Workshop Settings.

1. Who we are

Archwright is a single-developer workshop project. For any privacy question, write to dhfu94086@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through Archwright. The app makes no outbound network requests for analytics, telemetry, advertising, account sync, crash reporting, or remote storage.

  • Account / login: none required. There is no login flow.
  • Analytics / tracking SDKs: none. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Network calls: none initiated by the app. The "no network" posture is enforced by Apple's App Transport Security setting in the iOS app.
  • Server-side data: we operate no server for Archwright and therefore hold no data about you.

3. Data stored on your device

The following items are written to your device's local storage and never leave it:

  • Saved bridge entries: your gallery logbook, including bridge blueprint, biome, score, notes, and timestamps. Persisted in ArchwrightData/Galleries/bridges.json.
  • Progress and stats: total bridges built, wins, win rate, best score, biome tour progress, and unlocked badges. Persisted in ArchwrightData/.
  • Photo attachments: only when you explicitly choose to attach a photo (camera capture or library pick). Files are stored in ArchwrightData/Photos/ on your device. The original remains in your photo library.

4. Permissions

Archwright requests the following iOS permissions only at the moment they are needed. Both can be denied and the rest of the app still works.

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): used solely to capture a photo to attach to a saved bridge entry.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): used solely to pick an image to attach to a saved bridge entry.
  • Photo Library Add (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): used solely when you export a finished bridge photo back to your photo library.

5. Children

Archwright does not target children and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app contains no third-party content, ads, social features, or chat.

6. Erasing your data

You can delete every bridge, photo, stat, and badge by opening Workshop Settings → Erase All Workshop Data. The action is irreversible and removes only the data Archwright itself stores on your device. Any photo you previously saved to your iOS photo library via the export button remains in your library unless you delete it there.

7. Data security

Data lives in the iOS app sandbox. We do not transmit it anywhere. Standard iOS device protection (passcode, Face ID, encrypted backup) applies. We do not perform keylogging, fingerprinting, or tracking of any kind.

8. International users

Because Archwright does not transmit data off your device, your data does not cross international borders as a result of using the app. If you email support, your message and our reply travel through the email service you choose.

9. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we will revise the "Effective date" above. If a change is material we will surface a notice inside the app on next launch.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email dhfu94086@gmail.com. We respond within two business days.
